Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Overview

QX Connect is an iOS VPN client that connects to a Shadowsocks server you configure. We do not operate a proxy server and do not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Your configured server address, port, encryption method, and proxy rules are stored on your device. Your Shadowsocks password is stored in the iOS Keychain. This information is used only to configure the connection on your device.

VPN traffic

When connected, network traffic is routed according to the rules you configure and may be sent through your chosen Shadowsocks server. We do not collect, receive, or store your browsing history, VPN traffic, server password, or proxy-rule configuration. Your server provider may process traffic independently; review that provider's privacy practices.

Advertising

When the VPN is stopped and ads have not been removed, QX Connect may show banner ads provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may process information to provide, measure, and protect advertising according to the Google Privacy Policy. QX Connect does not share VPN traffic, server credentials, or browsing history with the advertising service.

Purchases

QX Connect offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads permanently. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment-card information. See the Apple Privacy Policy for details.

Children

QX Connect is not directed to children under the age required to consent to data processing in their country or region.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
