Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Overview

QX Connect is an iOS VPN client that connects to a Shadowsocks server you configure. We do not operate a proxy server and do not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Your configured server address, port, encryption method, proxy rules, and smart-routing preference are stored in the app's local container and shared App Group container. Your Shadowsocks password is stored in the iOS Keychain.

QX Connect also stores a single integer, capped at 20, representing the number of successful free VPN connections. This integer is used only to enforce the free-connection allowance. It is stored in the iOS Keychain, is not tied to an account, and is never uploaded. The Keychain item is restricted to this device and may remain after reinstalling the app on the same device; it does not migrate to another device.

Your acknowledgement of the in-app VPN data disclosure is stored in local app preferences. QX Connect does not provide user accounts and does not send any of this locally stored information to us.

VPN traffic

When the VPN is active, the iOS Packet Tunnel extension temporarily processes network packets, domain names, IP addresses, ports, and DNS requests on your device. This processing is necessary to apply the routing and proxy rules you selected and to operate the VPN connection.

We do not collect, store, record, inspect, sell, or use your browsing history, VPN traffic, DNS history, or packet contents for advertising, analytics, profiling, marketing, or any other secondary purpose.

Services selected by you

Traffic that matches your proxy rules, or traffic that smart routing retries through the proxy, is sent to the Shadowsocks server that you configure. QX Connect does not operate that server. The server operator and the websites, apps, or other destinations you access may process traffic under their own privacy practices.

DNS requests for domains routed through your proxy may be sent through that proxy to Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1 DNS resolver. Other DNS requests use the DNS service normally available to your device. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

No advertising, tracking, or analytics

QX Connect contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. QX Connect does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell or disclose VPN-related data to third parties for any purpose.

Purchases

QX Connect offers an optional, one-time non-consumable purchase that unlocks unlimited connections. Apple processes the transaction through StoreKit and may provide QX Connect with a cryptographically verified entitlement and transaction information needed to unlock or restore the purchase. We do not receive or store your payment-card information. See the Apple Privacy Policy for details.

Retention and deletion

Configuration and preference data in the app containers is removed by iOS when the app is deleted. Keychain items, including the server password and the successful-connection count, may remain on the same device after the app is deleted and reinstalled. We have no server-side copy of this information. A full device erase removes device-only Keychain items. You may replace the saved server password by saving a new configuration.

Children

QX Connect is not directed to children, does not create user profiles, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes affecting VPN data handling will also be disclosed in the app before use.
